# Backlink & Review Acquisition Plan — Aadikarta

Context: the marketing audit (2026-07-16) found zero third-party reviews, press mentions, or backlinks anywhere on the open web for Aadikarta — a general search for "aadikarta astrology reviews" returns nothing. This is a separate problem from the rendering bug (now fixed): even a perfectly crawlable site needs external signals to rank and to be trusted by users comparing platforms. This plan is manual outreach — I can't submit these on your behalf, but every entry below is ready to execute.

## Tier 1 — Free, high-authority directory listings (do these first, ~2-3 hours total)

These are the fastest wins: free, no editorial gatekeeping, and they're what AstroTalk/Astroyogi already have that Aadikarta doesn't.

| Platform | Action | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Create a listing for Aadikarta (category: "Astrologer" or "Online service") | Enables Google reviews, which directly feed local/brand search trust signals |
| Justdial | Free business listing | Highest-traffic India-specific directory for services like this |
| Sulekha | Free business listing under "Astrology Services" | Same category as competitors, direct comparison exposure |
| IndiaMART (if positioning as a service provider) | Free listing | High DA, India-focused |
| Trustpilot | Claim/create a business profile | Free reviews platform, works even pre-launch with a handful of seed reviews |
| Product Hunt | Launch post for the AI Astrologer feature specifically | Free backlink + relevant to a tech-literate audience that shares/upvotes |
| AlternativeTo | Add Aadikarta as an "AstroTalk alternative" and "Astroyogi alternative" | Directly captures the comparison-intent traffic the new /vs pages target |
| Crunchbase | Free company profile | Standard trust signal, low effort |

## Tier 2 — Review seeding (needs real users, ~ongoing)

Zero reviews anywhere is itself a red flag to both users and algorithms. Once there's any live user base:
1. Add a simple post-consultation prompt ("Rate your reading") that links to the Google Business Profile review link.
2. Seed Trustpilot the same way — a short follow-up email/SMS after a paid consultation with a direct review link converts far better than a cold ask.
3. Target: 10-20 genuine reviews before doing any paid PR or guest-post outreach — reviews are the credibility layer that makes everything else land.

## Tier 3 — Guest content / press outreach (higher effort, do after Tier 1)

| Target type | Example outlets | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Indian lifestyle/wellness blogs | HerZindagi, iDiva, Mid-Day lifestyle section | "How online astrology consultations work" contributed piece with a link back |
| Astrology-adjacent niche blogs | Any active Vedic astrology / Jyotish blogger with a Domain Rating >20 | Offer a free expert consultation in exchange for an honest review + link |
| Startup/India-tech press | YourStory, Inc42 (comment sections / smaller feature roundups) | Position the AI astrologer as the differentiator — "AI meets Jyotish" is a genuinely fresh angle for a tech-press roundup |
| Reddit / Quora | r/india, r/AskIndia astrology threads; Quora "best astrology app India" questions | Answer genuinely, disclose affiliation, link only when relevant — this is reputation-building, not spam |

## Sequencing

1. **This week:** Tier 1 directory listings (free, no dependencies, ~2-3 hours).
2. **Ongoing from first paying users:** Tier 1 review-seeding flow (Google/Trustpilot prompts).
3. **Once 10-20 reviews exist:** Tier 3 outreach — reviews make the guest-post pitch credible instead of cold.
4. **Revisit in 4-6 weeks:** re-run a `site:aadikarta.org` search and a brand-name search to check whether any of this has moved the needle before investing further.
